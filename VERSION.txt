# 1.5.74

## Discipline diagnostics and offline tests (fork)

### New: `/wfr disc` (also `/wfr diag`)
A read-only diagnostics window for Priests, made for the first login on the live WoW Forever client. Copy it with Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C. It never casts and never changes settings.

It shows:
- Client version / interface number next to the addon TOC interface (spot a launch-day interface change at once).
- Mana and health as the client reports them, including values the client hides (secret values).
- Which unit the Discipline heals would target, the lowest party member, the tank unit, group aggro.
- Weakened Soul / Shield / Renew on the heal unit, Holy Fire / Shadow Word: Pain on the target, marked UNREADABLE when the client hides them.
- Wand (Shoot 5019): known, resolved name, on an action bar, wand equipped, auto-repeat state.
- Every step of the Discipline rotation and defense list: suggestable or not, and the reason (not learned, health above threshold, Weakened Soul, mana below wand floor, not on a bar, cooldown...).
- The current HUD queue and defense suggestion.

### Offline tests
- `tests/` (not loaded by the game): a small WoW client mock and 54 checks for the Discipline list, the diagnostics and a regression baseline proving Priest Damage / Heal suggestions are unchanged since 1.5.72.
- Run from the addon folder with Lua 5.1: `lua5.1 tests/run.lua`.

### Discipline damage now follows the Icy Veins guide
- **Holy Fire** only as the opener (target at 90% health or more), no longer re-cast every time the DoT drops.
- **Smite** and **Mind Blast** once per target, in the guide's order (Smite, then Mind Blast, then Shadow Word: Pain), then the **wand until the mob dies**. Both come back after 30 s on the same target for long fights; the 30 s are editable per row in the options (0 = no wait). A new target starts over.
- Heals, buffs, pre-pull Shield and the wand mana floor are unchanged.

### Unchanged
- Priest Damage / Heal and every other class.
- Still no combat log, still never casts for you.
