# 1.5.73

## Discipline Priest role (fork)

New Priest playstyle **Discipline** (`disc`) next to Damage and Heal. Pick it with the HUD role button or `/wfr role`. Damage, Heal and every other class are unchanged.

### Rotation (heals first, damage when nobody needs healing)
- **Power Word: Shield** before the pull (out of combat, hostile target selected) on your focus, else the party member marked Tank, else yourself.
- **Power Word: Shield** in combat below 80% health. Never suggested while **Weakened Soul** is readable on that unit.
- **Flash Heal** only as an emergency (below 35%).
- **Prayer of Healing** when 3+ party members (you included) are at or below 70%.
- **Penance** (if talented) below 75%, **Heal** below 70% (**Lesser Heal** until Heal is learned), **Renew** below 90% when missing. Greater Heal is in the list but off by default.
- Damage: **Holy Fire → Smite → Mind Blast → Shadow Word: Pain → Wand (Shoot)**. Smite is the filler and gives way to Mind Blast / Shadow Word: Pain when they are ready.
- Heal target: friendly mouseover / target / focus, else the lowest party member. Health and auras (Shield, Weakened Soul, Renew) are read on that same unit.
- Pure priority list: a heal comes back as long as its health gate is met.

### Mana management
- New option (General tab, Discipline card): **Wand below mana %** (default 40%). Below it only the wand is suggested for damage. 0 = off.
- Targets under 20% (trash) / 5% (raid boss) are finished with the wand.
- Shoot is only suggested with a wand equipped and never while it is already shooting (pressing Shoot again would stop the wand).

### Defense / buffs (Discipline)
- Power Word: Shield on yourself below 50%, Fade when you have group aggro or below 40%.
- Inner Fire, Power Word: Fortitude, Divine Spirit (if talented), Shadow Protection.

### Notes
- Spells you have not learned yet (level, talent) are skipped silently.
- Still no combat log, still never casts for you.
